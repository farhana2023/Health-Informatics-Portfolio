# Career Goals and Self-Reflection

**Course:** COMP 4064 – Career Planning and Portfolio Development, George Brown College (Fall 2026)

**Type:** Individual assignment

## Assignment brief
Create a career plan that connects my Health Informatics education to my target career. The plan sets out short- and long-term career goals using the SMART framework, and a self-reflection on my strengths and areas for growth, with strategies to develop professionally and prepare for the job market.

## What I covered
- **SMART career goals:** short-term (co-op and entry-level role) and long-term objectives in health informatics and business analysis
- **Self-reflection:** my strengths and weaknesses in relation to my target role
- **Development plan:** strategies to close skill gaps and build industry readiness

## Target role
Health Informatics Analyst / Health Business Analyst in Ontario healthcare

## Skills demonstrated
Goal setting (SMART), self-assessment, career planning, professional writing
